Support

How can we help?

If the app does not work as expected, or a quote needs a source correction, open an issue in the public support repository. Include your iOS version, app version, and reproduction steps—never personal or sensitive information.

Choose an issue type

Where can I download Daily Quote?

iOS 1.0 is now available. Download Daily Quote on the App Store; iOS 17.0 or later is required. The link opens the US storefront. Download availability depends on your Apple Account region.

Why did I not receive my daily reminder?

First make sure Daily Reminder is enabled in the app, then open “Settings → Notifications → Daily Quote” on your iPhone. Low Power Mode, Focus, and Scheduled Summary may also affect delivery time.

How do I buy, restore, or manage Plus?

View Apple App Store subscriptions on the Plus screen and use Restore Purchases for an active entitlement on the same Apple Account. To cancel or check renewal, open “Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.” Price, trial, territory, and renewal terms appear on the purchase confirmation sheet.

What happens after a refund or expiration?

Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. When an entitlement expires or is refunded, Daily Quote stops creating new Plus content. Existing local data remains available to view, export, or delete as explained in the app. Family Sharing availability depends on the App Store product and Apple Account status.

Do favorites and settings sync?

Not by default. Plus subscribers may explicitly enable private iCloud sync for favorites, preferences, history, notes, share templates, and collection progress in their private CloudKit database. AI interpretations are excluded. If iCloud is unavailable, the app continues using local data.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings in the app to export or delete your reading archive, AI interpretations, and share templates, or turn off private iCloud sync and clear local data. Deleting the app normally removes local data; private CloudKit records follow Apple’s iCloud data rules.

What is sent for AI interpretation?

Each request requires explicit consent. By default it includes only the current quote, language, and a question you type. A note of up to 2,000 characters is included only when you separately enable it for that request. Cloudflare AI Gateway forwards the request to DeepSeek V4. The note is not retained in our service logs, AI history, or iCloud. Results stay on your device and can be exported or deleted.

How do I report an incorrect author or source?

Open a support issue with the quote, its current attribution, and a reliable source link. Content accuracy reports receive priority.
