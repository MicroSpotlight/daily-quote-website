Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Updated: October 31, 2026

Daily Quote follows data minimization. The app requires no account, does not sell personal data, and does not track you across apps or websites. Essential content and subscription services use the network. AI requests require consent each time, and iCloud sync is enabled by you. Starting with version 1.1.1, usage analytics and crash diagnostics start when the app launches; version 1.1 still requires you to opt in.

1. Data in the app

Your interests, favorites, notes, personal mark, language and reading preferences, reminder time, reading history, and AI interpretations are stored on your device by default. Relevant content is sent only when you choose to use the online recommendation, AI, or feedback features described below; optional iCloud sync writes to your own private database. Deleting the app normally deletes local data.

2. Content update service

The app requests quote versions, incremental updates, or complete snapshots from a public content service hosted by Cloudflare. These requests do not contain your interests, favorites, notes, reading history, reminders, device identifiers, or account information. Like any internet connection, Cloudflare may process IP address, request time, URL, network routing, and system configuration for transport, security, and reliability. We use only sampled technical logs without quote or note text to investigate service errors and do not build user profiles. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Online recommendations are a separate feature that is off by default. Only after you enable it does the app send the current date and your explicitly selected topic or mood to the content service to choose a quote. It does not send favorites, notes, or reading history. You can turn online recommendations off in Settings at any time.

3. Plus, AI, and content feedback

The app uses RevenueCat to manage Plus purchases, restores, migration of existing purchases, and subscription updates. When subscription services start, RevenueCat processes an anonymous App User ID generated by its SDK, App Store purchase information, products, prices, expiration dates, and necessary app, operating system, and storefront metadata to verify access and analyze subscriptions. Purchases are linked to this identifier, which contains no name or email address. No Daily Quote account is required. RevenueCat receives no notes, favorites, interests, or personal marks; automatic device identifier collection and diagnostics are disabled. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

For paid content or AI access, the app uses the existing content API and sends an Apple-signed purchase credential to our Cloudflare-hosted service, without a RevenueCat anonymous user identifier. The service retains its existing Apple verification and necessary subscription-state records. Credentials travel over encrypted connections and are not written to logs; AI rate-limit records retain only a one-way hash of the subscription identifier. RevenueCat manages purchases, restores, and entitlement decisions in the app. Essential subscription processing is independent of Firebase analytics and diagnostics.

Every AI interpretation request requires explicit consent. By default, the request contains only the current quote, language, and a question you type. A note of up to 2,000 characters is included only when you separately enable “Include this note” and confirm that request. Cloudflare AI Gateway forwards the request to DeepSeek V4 to produce the response. The note is treated as untrusted input for that request only and is not used to infer sensitive attributes. Daily Quote does not write your note, question, or model response to its own database or logs; D1 records only whether a note was included for audit. AI interpretations remain on your device and can be exported or deleted.

If you explicitly submit a source correction or content report, its text, quote ID, and submission time are sent to the content service for human review. Offline reports wait on the device until a connection is available. They are not used for advertising or profiling.

4. Optional private iCloud sync

Only after you subscribe to Plus and explicitly enable private iCloud sync will favorites, preferences, reading history, notes, share templates, collection progress, and related deletion records be written to the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account. Apple manages that data under your iCloud settings. AI interpretations are excluded. You can turn sync off and clear local data in the app.

5. Notifications

If you enable a daily reminder, Daily Quote requests notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder settings and notification content are not sent to our servers. You can turn notifications off in the app or system settings.

6. Sharing

When you choose to share, content is passed to the iOS share sheet. A destination service handles it under that service’s privacy policy. Daily Quote does not receive the outcome of your share.

7. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Daily Quote includes no advertising SDK and does not track you across apps. RevenueCat provides essential subscription management and purchase analytics. Starting with version 1.1.1, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics start when the app launches to measure app usage and improve reliability. Their SDKs may process installation, app-instance and vendor identifiers, device, app and operating system information, screen and lifecycle events, crash reports, and coarse location inferred from IP address. In version 1.1, Firebase collection is off by default and can be enabled or disabled in Settings; version 1.1.1 and later no longer provide this switch. We do not supply Firebase with notes, favorites, interests, search content, purchase credentials, or advertising identifiers. See Firebase privacy information.

8. Support requests

If you contact support through GitHub Issues, GitHub hosts the content you choose to submit under its privacy policy. Never include personal or sensitive information in a public issue.

9. Children’s privacy

Daily Quote does not direct data collection toward children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

10. Policy updates

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date, and update App Store privacy information when required.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or Daily Quote, use the support page.
